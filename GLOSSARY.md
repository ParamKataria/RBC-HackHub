# Black Cultural Centre for Nova Scotia (BCCNS) Digital Archive

The digital archive preserving and interpreting the records, oral histories, and community lineages of African Nova Scotians, Canada's oldest Black communities.

## Language

**Catalog Record**:
A single Dublin Core archival entry loaded from the physical or digital collection, documenting an artifact, document, photograph, person, or community.
_Avoid_: Item, entity, document, entry

**Inquiry**:
A researcher's plain-language question submitted to the archive to discover relevant historical records and synthesize grounded facts.
_Avoid_: Prompt, question, query, search

**Claim**:
A single factual assertion made by the archive guide in response to an inquiry, strictly grounded in and cited against one or more retrieved Catalog Records.
_Avoid_: Sentence, statement, answer, paragraph

**Citation**:
An unambiguous reference linking a Claim to the specific Catalog Record ID from which the historical fact was drawn.
_Avoid_: Source link, reference, mark, footnote
